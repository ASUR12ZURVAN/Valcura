Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Valcura collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Valcura clinic communication and workflow service.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how the service is used, Valcura may process:

  • Patient or contact details such as name and phone number.
  • Clinic, hospital, and staff account details.
  • Messages, call-related events, appointment or enquiry information, and message delivery status.
  • Information provided through WhatsApp, MacroDroid, web forms, or connected spreadsheet services.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide clinic communication workflows, send requested WhatsApp messages, respond to enquiries, maintain message and workflow records, synchronize information with configured business tools, authenticate authorized users, and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

3. WhatsApp and Third-Party Services

Valcura uses the WhatsApp Cloud API and may connect to services configured by a clinic, including Google Sheets and MacroDroid. Information sent through those services is also subject to the relevant provider's privacy policy and terms. Valcura does not sell personal information or use WhatsApp contact information for unrelated advertising.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We may share information only with the clinic or organization that authorized the service, service providers that help us operate the application, connected providers selected by that organization, or when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of users.

5. Data Retention and Security

Information is retained only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, to meet operational or legal requirements, or as directed by the organization using Valcura. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls and protected service credentials. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You may ask the clinic or organization that uses Valcura to access, correct, export, or delete information associated with you. You may also opt out of non-essential communications by contacting the organization that sent the message. We may need to verify a request before completing it.

7. Children's Privacy

Valcura is intended for use by clinics and organizations and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without appropriate authorization.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the clinic or organization that uses Valcura to communicate with you. Authorized Valcura customers may use their existing support channel to contact the Valcura service operator.
